3Embedding space in 3D

Selected token
Click a point (or a token chip) to see its nearest neighbours.
    Each token's vector comes from which tokens it appears next to in the corpus (PPMI + SVD), squashed to 3 axes with PCA. Similar tokens land close together. Highlighted points are the tokens of your input text.

    Drag to rotate · use the toolbar (top-right of the plot) to zoom or pan · click legend items to hide a group.
